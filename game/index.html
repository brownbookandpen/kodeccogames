// Carriers: Built in Unity

Developer's Log

A single-player, open-world zombie survival game built in Unity, set on a huge island. This page tracks the build as it happens: what works, what broke and what is next. It is a work in progress, so nothing here is final and there is no release date.

What works today

The islandA large, hand-shaped island with towns, forest, fields and a walled capital that is permanently flooded.
MovingRun, crouch, crawl prone, slide, vault and climb ladders.
FightingMelee, firearms and bows.
RidingHorseback with its own HUD, a minimap and a weapon wheel.
Loot and inventorySearchable containers, a backpack and rarity colours.
WeatherRain, storms, fog and snow, with a day and night cycle.
CharactersA character creator with layered clothing and colours.
BuildingA build mode with a large palette of props.

Coming next

SurvivorsLiving settlements and factions with their own routines.
Bringing back the deadZombies are being reworked in a test scene before they return.
Base and gangClaim a base and recruit survivors.
Story and savesA story intro and a full save system.

A living weather system

Rain, storms and fog now roll in over about a minute. Ground, roofs and grass get wet, then dry out again when the sky clears.

Recent

The island, regrown

The whole island has new stylised grass and flower fields, with a dark spruce forest across the snowy north.

Recent

Ladders, crawling and a companion

You can now climb ladders and crawl through tall grass, and you get an outline when you are hidden. A white wolf-dog follows you, waits while you climb and comes back when you call.

Recent

Blood

Combat now leaves blood sprays and pools on the ground.
