Zombies now drop loot

Kills now leave loot on the ground, in the style of an action RPG. The corpse then sinks into the terrain and disappears, so nothing is taken from the body. Walk over the drops and press E to collect.

Draft

Riding, and zombies that lunge

The HUD and minimap stay visible on horseback, with gait and stamina shown. Zombies pick a random running style when they spawn, and aggressive ones can lunge at a player who is close and in view.
