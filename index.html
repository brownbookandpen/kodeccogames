Kodecco Games

One outbreak.
Two ways to survive it.

Kodecco Games is a small independent studio building the Carrier universe: a world after the outbreak, where the horde is the obvious danger and the people beside you are the quiet one.

Carrier, the card game, puts that world on the table. Five to twelve players, secret roles, and one question that never goes away: who among you is already infected?

Carriers, built in Unity, takes it somewhere you can walk around. A single-player survival game on a large island, where you scavenge, fight, ride and try to make it to the next day.

We build both in public. The dev logs are where we show our work, including the parts that didn't work.
