Roadmap

Where the project stands, and what's coming next.

PHASE 1

Concept & Core Loop ▾

Defined the horde mechanic and core survival loop. Completed.

First Draft
Idealisation
Support Kits
Border & Zombies

Midjourney was still pretty new back then, and I was curious enough to test it out on these early concepts — some of these backgrounds and assets are AI-generated because I simply couldn't paint scenes like this myself yet. It is what it is; just part of the trail that led to where Carrier's art is now. Our early playtests also ran WAY too long — each game dragged on forever, and that's when we knew we had to go back to the drawing board.

PHASE 2

This is also around when we started moving away from Midjourney and began drawing things ourselves — squeezing in art sessions during whatever free time we had. We still cut corners where we could (outfits especially), but from here on, the art was ours.

PHASE 3 — ONGOING

Balancing & Playtesting [OVERHAUL] ▾

Overhauling the game's mechanics — optimizing systems and cutting redundancies, not building a whole new game. Running structured playtests to tune difficulty and pacing along the way.

Characters
Secret Roles
Encounters
Special Conditions

We're deep in balancing right now — tightening up Characters, Secret Roles, Encounters, and Special Conditions. Some of it is trimming redundancy, some of it is fixing confusing naming, and some of it is just making sure every card earns its place in the deck.

PHASE 4 — ONGOING

Art & Component Design ▾

As our design skills grow, we're moving further from the original Midjourney/AI-assisted art — toward asset packs from itch.io, and eventually hiring dedicated pixel art artists.
