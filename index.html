One outbreak. Two ways to survive it.

Kodecco Games is a small independent studio building the Carrier universe: a world overrun by a horde, where some of the people next to you are already infected and nobody can tell which.

The universe lives in two games. One is a social deduction card game played around a table. The other is a survival game built in Unity. Both share the same survivors, the same horde and the same question: who can you trust?

We build in public. Pick a door to read the rules, follow the dev logs and see how each game is coming together.
